Citizens Abroad

November 20, 2026

For our citizenship is in heaven, from which we also eagerly wait for a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ;

(Phil 3:20 NASB)

There are people who travel to foreign countries in order to do things which are illegal in their home country. Usually these trips are to do something bad, such as sex or drugs. Sometimes it's to do something good, like find an experimental medical treatment or practice an outlawed faith. But whichever the case, they are engaging in behavior that is not allowed back home.

As citizens of heaven, we also are living in a foreign land, and we are surrounded by opportunities to "legally" live in a way that our king has forbidden. The culture of this world is ingrained with standards and beliefs which allow and even celebrate behaviors which our God finds reprehensible, including lying, sex, drugs and alcohol use, gossip, unfaithfulness, worrying, and much more. God calls us to go against the flow of this world. He asks us to be weirdos. If you've ever witnessed a foreigner do something strange, making you say or think, "That's not how we do things here," we should expect the people of this world to look at us that same way when we live abroad as citizens of our heavenly kingdom.

When God came to Sodom and Gomorrah, he found Lot living there. Despite the evil lifestyles of everyone else, Lot had not yet given in to their debauchery, and because of his faithful living he and his family were spared from the destruction that God brought. If God had discovered Lot living in the same manner as the locals, the end of that story would have been much more tragic for him.

When we decide how to spend our time, money, efforts, and attention, we must consider not just what the customs of this world are, but whether we are about to dishonor God. Sometimes the outcome of our decisions will diminish our reputations in people's eyes. We need to daily choose to reject human opinions and live only for God's opinion.

Consider:
  • Consider the differences between what's acceptable to a citizen of the world and a citizen of heaven. Have you compromised or forgotten the ways of your people?
  • Are you caught in the snare of worrying what people will think of you? Pray for deliverance from that, and find strength in the joy God has when you walk in His ways.
