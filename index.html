Increasing Our Thankfulness

November 26, 2026

Let the word of Christ richly dwell within you, with all wisdom teaching and admonishing one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing with thankfulness in your hearts to God.

(Col 3:16 NASB)

When I was a kid, it was hard for me to be thankful on Thanksgiving. Sure, I was thankful for the yummy food we were about to eat. But I lived my life always looking forward to getting that next thing. It got to the point where I wouldn't even use or play with the thing when I got it, I was just looking for the rush of getting it. I would spend a few days enjoying the feeling of having the thing, but when that feeling was gone, I would immediately start looking for the next thing to set my heart on. Someone who lives rush-to-rush like this can rarely be thankful for anything they have.

Thankfulness occurs when we take time to recognize the value of either what we have or what's been done for us, and the best way to recognize that value is to look at or remember those who don't have it, or to remember a time when we didn't have it, or to consider what life would be like without it. As I kid, I focused on what others had and what I didn't have. This is backwards!

One of the most important things we Christians can be thankful for is a salvation that was entirely outside of our grasp. Even though the religions and the religious people of the world think they can work to earn salvation, we recognize that everyone is truly helpless to save themselves, and that our only hope was for God to do the work to save us.

On this Thanksgiving, before someone corners you with the question about what you're thankful for, spend time genuinely reflecting on the blessings you have that others don't, and most of all recognize that God loved you so much that He purchased - by His blood - a salvation that you were in desperate need of yet could never have attained on your own.

Consider:
  • How much time do you spend remembering the value of the blessings you have versus the time you spend dreaming of the blessings you still want?
  • Do you recognize that salvation was entirely out of your grasp, that you were helpless to attain it on your own?
  • So many people around the world have never heard the name of the only one who can save them. Make time to pray for them today and every day. If praying for everyone feels too big, pick a nation or a people group and commit to praying for just them.
