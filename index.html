Not "What If", But "What Is"

November 28, 2026

When Israel was a youth I loved him, And out of Egypt I called My son. The more they called them, The more they went away from them; They kept sacrificing to the Baals And burning incense to idols. Yet it is I who taught Ephraim to walk, I took them in My arms; But they did not know that I healed them. I pulled them along with cords of a man, with ropes of love, And I became to them as one who lifts the yoke from their jaws; And I bent down and fed them.

(Hosea 11:1-4 NASB)

I once applied for a sales job with a nation-wide store. They had automated their interview process, and I was filling out an online personality survey. One of the questions was, "Do you have any regrets?" The question really took me off guard, and I spent a long time staring at it, trying to figure out why they would ask such a question. Don't we all have some regrets on some level? Can any of us say we responded to every situation exactly the way we wish we would have? I didn't get the job and I always wondered if my answer (or the time I took thinking of my answer) was the reason.

As Christians we sometimes get caught up in our "what ifs", especially regarding mistakes we've made and opportunities we've missed. We can think we've let God down, embarrassed and ashamed that we're not giving Him His "money's worth". But as the saying goes, "You can't let God down, because you were never holding Him up." Before even a single star was placed in the sky, God knew the outcome of every choice you ever did or ever will make, good, bad, and ugly. He doesn't have "buyer's remorse" about you, as if He were caught off guard about what kind of person you are. The story of your entire life - beginning, middle, and end - was written in a book on His shelf before your very first breath. He purchased you with His blood knowing exactly what He was getting.

Instead of "what if", let us focus on "what is": you are loved at the highest level God is capable of loving you, and you are separated from your sins as far as God is capable of separating you from them. You are not a restored, second-hand creation, you are a new creation. He's not embarrassed by you; He gushes over you! He has put you in a place of prominence on His shelf, right next to all the other messy human lives He's made new.

So stop projecting the disappointment you've received from mere humans onto the God of perfect love. Imagine Him hugging you tightly, delighting just to be in this moment with you, singing over you as He patiently wipes clean your dirty face. He is neither oblivious to your faults nor impeded by them.

Consider:
  • Do you struggle to come to God because of who you think you are?
  • Can God continue to be angry against someone whose sins have been completely removed?
  • Can you reimagine God as one who sings over you, gushes over you, delights in you?
