No Turning Back

November 22, 2026

In My Father's house are many rooms; if that were not so, I would have told you, because I am going there to prepare a place for you. And if I go and prepare a place for you, I am coming again and will take you to Myself, so that where I am, there you also will be.

(John 14:2-3 NASB)

When the FAA nationwide ground stop occurred last week, the people who were already in the air let out a collective sigh of relief. Even though any plane which hadn't taken off yet was forced to stay grounded, anyone already on their way to their destination could continue on their way. Can you imagine the surprise and relief you would have felt learning you were on the last plane out that day, just narrowly escaping misfortune? Or the frustration of being out on the runway, moments away from takeoff just to hear the pilot say he had to turn back?

The second you trusted in Jesus, He began building and decorating your room in His father's house. He's picked out the curtains and the furniture, He's painting the walls, and He's stocking the fridge. The reason He's going to all this trouble is because your arrival is no longer a matter of if, but when. Like the people on those airplanes, you might not yet be at your destination, but you are safely on your way.

One day, God will call a divine "ground stop", and anyone who hasn't yet chosen the heavenly transport will no longer have the opportunity to reach their destination. He has made enough room on our flight for everyone we know, and He doesn't want anyone to be left behind (2 Peter 3:9). His plan is for us to share the good news of what we've found with everyone not yet onboard, convincing them to get out while they can. The best part of this news is that no one gets bumped or asked to give up their seat; your plane departs the moment you trust in Him, and it doesn't stop until it arrives at your destination. "The vilest offender who truly believes, that moment from Jesus a pardon receives."

Consider:
  • Do you struggle with fear of not making it in? "...whoever believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life." (John 3:16)
  • Who in your life needs your fervent prayers to be brought onboard the heavenly flight?
