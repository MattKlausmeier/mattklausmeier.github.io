Sometimes It's A Miracle

November 17, 2026

And ordering the crowds to sit down on the grass, He took the five loaves and the two fish, and looked up toward heaven. He blessed the food and breaking the loaves, He gave them to the disciples, and the disciples gave them to the crowds. And they all ate and were satisfied, and they picked up what was left over of the broken pieces: twelve full baskets. There were about five thousand men who ate, besides women and children.

(Matthew 14:19-21 NASB)

We often see God's provision for us coming through ordinary means. He provides us with a job and a paycheck, He's given us doctors and medicine, we have police, grocery stores, family, and friends, and through all of these things we see our needs met.

But at certain times in life, these ordinary means of provision fall through. Maybe we lose our job, or maybe the doctor tells us he can't do anything. Maybe our cell phone is dead and we're in the middle of nowhere. In these moments we might be tempted to think that all hope is lost. We might look around and see no logical way that we can be rescued from our situation.

Today's verse reminds us that "no logical way" doesn't stop God from providing for us. God can turn one loaf of bread into a thousand, He can bring water out of a rock, He can make one jar of oil fill up hundreds of others, and He can make disease disappear in an instant. We've surrounded ourselves with so many layers of protection that we don't often get the opportunity for God to bless us supernaturally, and we panic in these situations because we've forgotten just how powerful He is.

Today if your find yourself in a "hopeless" situation, recall to your mind all the amazing stories of when God miraculously provided for His people, and use those stories to power your prayers as you ask for His provision with a believing heart.

Consider:
  • Are you facing an "impossible" situation?
  • Has God ever brought you out of an "impossible" place before?
  • Keep a journal of answered prayers so that you don't forget all that He's done for you.
