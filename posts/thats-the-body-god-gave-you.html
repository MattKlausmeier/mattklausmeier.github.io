That's The Body God Gave You

November 27, 2026

For You created my innermost parts; You wove me in my mother's womb. I will give thanks to You, because I am awesomely and wonderfully made; Wonderful are Your works, And my soul knows it very well. My frame was not hidden from You When I was made in secret, And skillfully formed in the depths of the earth; Your eyes have seen my formless substance; And in Your book were written All the days that were ordained for me, When as yet there was not one of them.

(Ps 139:13-16 NASB)

You'd probably have a hard time finding someone who has no improvements they'd like to see made to their body. Some of us have health issues we would like to see gone. Many more of us would take a nip here or a tuck there, more hair and less wrinkles. After yesterday's feasting some of us just wish we were still on the same belt notch! Very few can claim to have attained the peak of health and beauty that we long for, and those who have are now in a battle against the natural progression of age.

But despite all our flaws, the bodies we have are exactly the bodies God gave us. This can be a difficult reality for some of us to accept, especially those of us with serious health conditions. But the fact remains: when your body was being formed, God said, "Yes" or "No" to every possible outcome. If not one sparrow falls to the ground apart from God's will (Matt 10:29), then His sovereignty even directed the very gamete which fertilized the egg in your mother's womb. You are awesomely and wonderfully made! God's works are wonderful and you are His handiwork!

But how do we reconcile the difficulties some of us face from our health or appearance with God's love? The answer is found in Romans 8:28: "And we know that God causes all things to work together for good to those who love God, to those who are called according to His purpose." If you are a believer, then despite - or perhaps because - of your body, you came to God, and perhaps many more will, too. Who can say if with better health or a better appearance you would now be a part of His family? Fanny Crosby was made blind as an infant due to bad medical advice, yet she went on to memorize entire books of the Bible, enabling her to write over 8,000 gospel hymns and songs, including "To God Be the Glory", "Safe in the Arms of Jesus", and "Blessed Assurance".

A day is coming when you will be given a body of indescribable beauty and perfect health. But as for today, have faith that for the purposes God planned for your life, written in His book before the first of them came to pass, you have a body hand-crafted by the God of love, and through it He will do glorious good!

Consider:
  • Does something about your body upset you? Practice praying, "God, I don't understand, but I trust you and your love for me, that you will keep your promise to work all things together for good."
  • Do you have hurt or anger towards God you might lay down today?
  • Have you held any unchristian thoughts or feelings towards another because of their appearance or lack of self-care? It's time to repent of that.
