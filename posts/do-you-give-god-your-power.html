Do You Give God Your Power?

November 24, 2026

... For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.

(Matt 6:13b KJV)

(While Biblical scholars disagree about whether the above verse was part of the original Biblical manuscripts, all agree that the kingdom, the power, and the glory belong to God.)

While none of us has God level power, or even superhero level power, each of us has a form of power as it comes to humans. We have time. We have money. We have strength and health. We have a mind that can think and some level of education. These are our power. And all of these things, whether we were born with them or were given them or worked hard and earned them, ultimately came from God. This entire universe was made by Him, and when a part of it comes into our grasp, it's because it was put into our hands by Him.

But while it is wisdom to recognize that all we have comes from God, we must also see the other thing He gives to us, the other side of this coin: He brings to us the opportunities to use these resources for His purposes. "For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand so that we would walk in them." (Eph 2:10 NASB) God gives us opportunities to use the resources He has given us, and it is a sin not to: "So for one who knows the right thing to do and does not do it, for him it is sin." (Jas 4:17 NASB) We would be rightly offended if the bank told us we could put our money in, but we cannot take it out. We would be rightly offended if we paid a worker to do the job but he took the money and ran. And God is robbed of what He is due when we don't accomplish His will with our resources of time, strength, money, talent, and energy.

Do you realize that our time in this world is fleeting, and that no matter how much or how little you acquire while you're here, you will lose every bit of it the moment you step into eternity? The people of this world build up their wealth and power here because they have no other choice, but we do: "Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth, where moth and rust destroy, and where thieves break in and steal. But store up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust destroys, and where thieves do not break in or steal..." (Matt 6:19-20 NASB) Every time you use what you have for God and His kingdom you're making a "currency exchange", converting your expiring earthly resources into eternal riches. "Only one life, 'twill soon be past; only what's done for Christ will last." (C. T. Studd)

Consider:
  • Are you more concerned with storing up earthly riches or heavenly ones?
  • Do you believe that God is able to provide you with more than you could ever give away?
  • If money isn't your earthly "power", what other resources do you have? If you're confined to a bed, why not pray for people or write them encouraging notes? If you have a car, who needs a ride? If you can cook, who needs a meal?
