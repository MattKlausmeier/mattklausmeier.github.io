God Is Waiting For Them

November 18, 2026

The Lord is not slow about His promise, as some count slowness, but is patient toward you, not willing for any to perish, but for all to come to repentance.

(2 Peter 3:9 NASB)

Have you ever had an unbelieving friend ask you why God could allow all the evil in the world to continue? Even if you don't know someone who feels that way, we've all heard someone share that sentiment in movies or on the internet. They are perplexed that God could see all the suffering of mankind and not step in and put it to an end.

The beautiful answer to their question is that God is waiting for them! They're the reason He hasn't wrapped it all up yet, because once He does they will be forever shut out of His kingdom, and that's the last thing He wants. God holds off on addressing the world's evil because there are more He wants to save. In Genesis 7 we read how the humans and the animals boarded the ark right up until the last second, and then right as the flooding began God closed the door behind them, permanently separating the saved from the lost.

If you're bothered by the suffering we see around the world, and if you are eager for Jesus to return and bring us home into His paradise, why not do your part to speed Him along by sharing His good news with the lost? You can either be directly involved with evangelism, or if that's not your gift, you can give financially to support the missionaries who are doing His work. And of course, we should all remember every day to "plead with the Lord of the harvest to send out workers into His harvest." (Matt 9:38) Your prayers can send workers, supply the workers' needs, and protect those workers who hasten the day of the Lord.

Consider:
  • Suffering exists in every corner of the world. Does your heart hurt for the hurting? Pray for a new heart if it doesn't.
  • How are you involved in reaching the lost? Can you name specific people who will be in the kingdom because of your evangelism, prayers, or giving?
  • Find a missionary to adopt. Pray for them, support them, and communicate encouragement and love to them.
