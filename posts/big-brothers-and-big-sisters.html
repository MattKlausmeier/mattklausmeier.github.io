Big Brothers And Big Sisters

November 19, 2026

Actually, then, it is already a defeat for you, that you have lawsuits with one another. Why not rather suffer the wrong? Why not rather be defrauded? On the contrary, you yourselves do wrong and defraud. And this to your brothers and sisters!

(1 Cor 6:7-8 NASB)

I had a friend who was the oldest of six. Whenever I visited I would find him entertaining his siblings, playing with them, feeding them, and changing their diapers. He was a fantastic example of what a big brother should be. When he was about 10 or 11, I remember watching "The Prince of Egypt" with him, and during the scene when baby Moses' older sister was carrying him down to the river in a basket, watching over him as he floated down river, my friend started to tear up. When I asked him why, he said, "She's such a good big sister!" Many of us wish we had big brothers and sisters like that.

Do you realize that the other people in your church are your brothers and sisters? God has adopted us all and made us His children, making the people we see at church every Sunday our spiritual brothers and sisters. My question to you this morning is: are you being a good big brother/big sister to your spiritual siblings? Or are you bossy, always telling them to try harder but never helping? Are you a pest, never giving them a chance to do it on their own? Are you simply uninvolved, not caring about them and their progress?

Some of us are so good about showing up for our biological and extended families no matter what crisis they're going through, but we don't consider our church-mates to be just as deserving of our familial love. Some of us who would never divorce our spouses or abandon our children are just one bad Sunday away from walking out on our church family. I know it's scary to think about opening up and getting involved with that many people at the level you care for your family, but if you can get enough people in your church to get on board with this very Biblical idea, there will never be a shortage of people to care for you or each other. "By this all people will know that you are My disciples: if you have love for one another." (John 13:35 NASB)

Consider:
  • When you look around at church, do you see family? Practice doing this.
  • What gifts do you have, spiritual or worldly, which you could use to bless your brothers and sisters?
  • You can't bless them if you don't know them. Start by picking just one new person to introduce yourself to, but don't just exchange names; really try to learn about them!
