Dream Chaser

November 3, 2026

Come now, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we will go to such and such a city, and spend a year there and engage in business and make a profit." Yet you do not know what your life will be like tomorrow. For you are just a vapor that appears for a little while, and then vanishes away. Instead, you ought to say, "If the Lord wills, we will live and also do this or that." But as it is, you boast in your arrogance; all such boasting is evil.

(James 4:13-16 NASB)

Your word is a lamp to my feet And a light to my path.

(Psalms 119:105 NASB)

Have you ever been out on a dark night and seen a little pinprick of light, way off in the distance? Here in Colorado we have beautiful mountains, and on the clearest nights, you can sometimes see tinys point of light coming from up on those mountains. These little lights can be a metaphor for our hopes and dreams for the future. And my simple message for you today is this: it's okay to walk towards those lights, but never by those lights.

When we carry the light of Christ and His word, then we will see clearly where we step. And we are free to continue this journey, by His light, towards our dreams. But when we set down His light, and run off into the night, chasing our little dream lights without Him, this is when we smack our faces in branches, stumble over rocks, and fall into ravines. Abandoning the way of Christ to chase riches, success, or any other dream is idolatry.

In the news recently was a story about a woman who landed her dream job, but then gave it up almost immediately because she realized how much of her time it would rob from her family. She could have turned her back on her kids, running off into the darkness, but she chose the way of Christ. And so should we all.

Consider:
  • Consider your goals and dreams, and ask yourself how you would feel if they never came to be. Could you easily shrug and say, "The Lord didn't will it, and I'm okay with that"? Or would you feel your life falling apart? The latter is a symptom of idolatry.
  • When you illuminate your path with God's word, are there certain dreams which are no longer reachable? Perhaps a business dream which would mean sacrificing your family? A hobby which divides your time and attention, robbing your employer of your best efforts? A level of status which discourages you from sharing God's truth?
