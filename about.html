About Me

Hi, I'm Matthew Klausmeier. I grew up in Indiana (which is what you see as the background image for this site), but currently I live in Colorado where I work as a computer programmer. Ever since high school I've gone back and forth between jobs involving ministry and those involving computers. On the ministry side, I've been a youth pastor, an interim pastor, a missionary, and I spent a couple of years planning and running Christian summer camps for over 500 kids. On the computer side, I've made video games, websites, apps, and even secret stuff for the government (it's much more boring than it sounds).
My degree is in computer programming and networking, with an emphasis on video games; I haven't attended seminary. But during high school I developed a hunger for good Christian teaching, and since then I've read or listened to everything I could get my hands on. I joined YWAM in 1998 where my eyes were opened to the world of missions, and I've enjoyed many good books and podcasts from men such as Tim Chaddick, Charles Spurgeon, Alistair Begg, Francis Chan, and others.
I was a teenager the first time someone told me I have a gift for teaching the Word of God, and it's something people have continued to affirm throughout my life. So even though I'm employed by day as a mild mannered computer programmer, I publish these blog posts daily as a way to strengthen and encourage you, my brothers and sisters in Christ.
Please reach out to let me know if my words have been of any encouragement to you!
