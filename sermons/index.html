Sermons

In 2012, Carmel Friends Church was waiting for our incoming pastor to get his visa and arrive from Australia. I was asked to fill in as the Interim Pastor while we waited for him. We thought it would be a two month process; it turned out to be a two year process!

I did my best to pass along the great lessons and teachings I've received over the years, but Disclaimer: I'm not a trained pastor! It's possible you might disagree with what I said or how I said it. (What's "expository preaching"?) I've learned a lot since I gave these sermons and it's possible I've grown and changed in my understanding of what I said. Feel free to reach out and ask for clarification. Nevertheless, I still find a lot of good encouragement and reminders as I look over what I said. I hope you do, too!

(Also, the docx files are AI's best attempt to listen to these audio files and transcribe them, but there's a lot of errors in there! If something looks wrong, check the audio file to see what I really said.)
